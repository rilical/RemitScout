# About Page Images

**Upload your 800x800px headshot here:**

- **Filename**: `omar-ghabayen-headshot.jpg`
- **Dimensions**: 800x800px (square/1:1 aspect ratio)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: Keep under 500KB for optimal performance

### Optimal Photo Guidelines:
- Professional or semi-professional headshot
- Clear, well-lit face
- Solid or simple background
- Centered composition
- Natural expression

The About page at `/about` will automatically use this image when you upload it.
